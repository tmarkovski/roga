The second update to Roga, with submodules you can see and update, hunks you can stage from the diff, an Inline view of the whole file, and a history that keeps up with checkouts. 0.0.3 and 0.0.4 were tagged but never released, so their changes are here too.

## What's new

- **Submodules.** A submodule's row says how its pointer moves, and its diff lists the submodule's own commits between the two. Under Unstaged, one that's behind has Update and one with new commits has Stage. The refs panel has a Submodules section after Stashes, where each row shows the checked-out commit and how it stands, and clicking one shows it in the panels under history.
- **Stage a hunk.** In an unstaged diff, Stage Hunk shows on a hunk's band after Discard Hunk, and in a staged diff Unstage Hunk does. Right-clicking a hunk offers the same.
- **Inline view.** The diff panel can show the whole file with the changes in place, from a button in its header or View › Diff View.
- **Hunk bands.** Each hunk starts with a band of two rows: a little space, then git's header in bold over a line. Conflicts are headed the same way, and the bands' buttons stay clear of the scroller and in place as the window resizes.
- **Discard File** is in the diff panel's header, before Stage File. It says Move to Trash for an untracked file.
- **Missing commits.** When the checked-out branch is behind its upstream, history fades the commits a pull would bring in.
- **Replay** has Last 3 days and the newest 10 to 200 commits in its Range menu, opens on a title card, and a saved video gets its last frame as its cover.

## Smaller changes

- History loads again as soon as a ref moves, and keeps its rows and text on screen while it does, so a checkout no longer makes it blink.
- A click selects a history row as the button goes down, which makes the highlight show sooner.
- Undo Commit refuses once the commit has been pushed, and says to revert it from history instead.
- After a commit, discard or pull, the title row keeps the counts in view beside what happened.
- The stage and discard buttons in the lists and the diff header are plain words until the pointer is over them.
- In Roga's menus a branch's name is bold, in place of the branch icon. Replay history has a group of its own.
- A branch's row no longer has a compare button. Review it from its menu or with Shift-Command-R.
- The uncommitted changes panel's header drops its discard button, since Discard All… sits under each list.
- The refs panel's rows no longer show behind the title row's buttons when scrolled, and the Remotes plus lines up with the counts.
- Roga raises its open-file limit as it starts, so many repositories in the sidebar no longer make git commands fail or crash the app.
