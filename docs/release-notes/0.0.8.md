The fifth update to Roga, with a repository's worktrees as tabs along the top of the window, the branch and its commands in a row of their own under them, and a top row that Roga draws itself at 44 points.

## What's new

- **Worktrees as tabs.** The row along the top of the window shows the selected repository's worktrees as tabs: the repository's own folder first, then a tab for each linked worktree, then a + for a new one. Each tab has a mark for an agent or for conflicts, the branch's name with the part up to its last slash in gray, and the most pressing number, such as uncommitted files or commits to pull. Command-1 to Command-9 go to a tab, and Control-Tab and Control-Shift-Tab go to the next and the previous one. On hover, a … button opens the same menu as the worktree's row in the sidebar, and a right-click opens it too.
- **A row for the branch.** The branch's chip and counts, Fetch, New Branch and history's number of commits and order move from the top row to a row of their own under the tabs, as tall as a panel's header and in line with the refs panel's.
- **A sidebar of repositories.** Since the tabs list the worktrees, the sidebar now lists only repositories, and a repository's row stands for all its worktrees. A click goes back to the worktree you showed last, and the row's count and mark are for the most urgent of them. To list worktrees in the sidebar as before, turn on Show worktrees in the menu beside Repositories, or View › Show Worktrees in Sidebar.

## Smaller changes

- The top row is 44 points tall, as tall as a panel's header, and Roga draws it instead of using macOS's toolbar. The traffic lights and the sidebar's button stay in the window's top left corner whether the sidebar shows or not.
- Dragging any empty part of the top row moves the window. A double-click does what you chose in System Settings only in the row's top 32 points, and does nothing below them.
- Panel headers are 44 points too, with 32-point buttons and 16-point icons, and the search field, the branch's chip and its counts grow to match.
- A button under the pointer has a fill of its own, darker than a row's, so a button at the end of a hovered row stands out: 10% black in the light theme and 15% white in the dark, and a step stronger while it's pressed.
